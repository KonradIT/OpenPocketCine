Field Monitor · Open Source

The open field
monitor for Osmo Pocket.

Pair over Bluetooth, join the camera's Wi-Fi, and watch a live HEVC (or AVC) feed with professional scopes and assists. Free, Apache-2.0, no vendor SDK.

Get the source How to connect

Built for Osmo Pocket 4 / 4 Pro and Nano. iOS first; Android lives in the same repository.

Scopes

Read the image like a colorist.

Waveform and RED-inspired Traffic Lights sit on the live Pocket feed, next to the picture you are judging.

Control

Keep the camera in the rig.

ISO, shutter, zoom, gimbal, and record from the phone — including Auto D-Log switching so zoom still works.

Portrait

Vertical shooting, full assist rail.

LUT, peaking, false color, zebra, waveform, parade, histogram, vectorscope, Traffic Lights, guides, grid, and crosshair — all in the portrait layout.

Free. Open source. Yours

No subscriptions, no telemetry.

OpenPocketCine is Apache-2.0 licensed and built in public. Inspect the protocol core, improve the monitor, and keep the tool you rely on.

This project is not affiliated with DJI. I learned BLE pairing and camera Wi-Fi connection with the help of Osmosis by Konrad Iturbe, and I'm grateful. The field-monitor architecture follows OpenZCine. Verify record start/stop on the camera body until you trust the link.
