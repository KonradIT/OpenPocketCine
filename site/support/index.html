OpenPocketCine pairs over Bluetooth, joins the camera's Wi-Fi, and shows a live HEVC or AVC monitor. iOS needs a physical iPhone — the Simulator has no BLE or camera SoftAP.

01

Connect a camera

  1. Power on the Osmo Pocket and keep DJI Mimo closed so it does not steal the link.
  2. In OpenPocketCine, scan for the camera over Bluetooth and approve pairing on the camera screen.
  3. Allow Local Network and Bluetooth when iOS asks. The app then joins the camera Wi-Fi.
  4. Wait for the connection phase to reach Connected. Battery and storage readouts mean the datalink is up.

Never paste camera Wi-Fi passwords into GitHub issues.
